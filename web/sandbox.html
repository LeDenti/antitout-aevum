How it is wired

A small relayer holds the keys of this one agent (an ECDSA session key and an ML-DSA-65 key). It seals the agent's decision and sends it; it cannot do more than the vault lets this agent do: 0.02 tUSD8 per payment, 0.10 per day, two known payees. Three refused acts put the agent in quarantine for two minutes. The seal is checked here, in your browser, against the key hash the owner registered on-chain.
